## 2. The free tools

The tools (HR diagnostic, performance evaluation form, career ladder, recruiting
kit, engagement survey) run in your browser. When you press *generate*, the
following is sent to us:

- the choices you made in the tool,
- the document the tool generated, and
- the company name, if you entered one (optional).

We use this only to understand which tools people use and how to improve them.
Please do not enter personal information about individual employees into the
tools.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in improving the
  tools. You can avoid any transfer by not generating, and avoid identifying your
  company by leaving the company field empty.
- **How it reaches us:** the data is delivered to us as a message through
  Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc. / Telegram FZ-LLC, outside the EU).
  [[REVIEW: see the note at the end of this draft.]]
- **Retention:** [[e.g. deleted after 12 months]].

## 3. Studio enquiries

If you send an enquiry through the Studio form, we receive your name, email
address, company, team size, timeline, the tools you use, your needs and any
notes you add. We use them only to reply and to prepare a possible project.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps taken at your request before a
  possible contract.
- **How it reaches us:** as a Telegram message, as in section 2.
- **Retention:** [[e.g. deleted 12 months after our last contact, unless a
  project follows]].

## 4. Emailing us

If you email us, we process your email address and what you write in order to
reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We delete the correspondence when
it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law.

## 5. Buying a product

Paid products are sold through Gumroad, Inc., which acts as the merchant of
record: it processes your payment, handles tax, and delivers the file. Its
privacy policy applies to the checkout: https://gumroad.com/privacy. Gumroad
shares your name, email address and order details with us so that we can
provide support and keep records required by law.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the purchase) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal
  record-keeping).
- **Retention:** for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## Note for the owner (delete before publishing)

**Telegram is the weak point.** It is outside the EU, it is not covered by an EU
adequacy decision, and there is no data processing agreement with it. That is
hard to justify under the GDPR for Studio enquiries, which contain names and
email addresses. The cleanest fix: once `hello@acuolo.com` has a real inbox,
send these notifications by email instead (or to an EU-hosted service), and
update sections 2 and 3. Until then, the tool notifications carry little or no
personal data, but the Studio form does.
