How Acuolo works

Most companies run on spreadsheets, docs and wikis, a database tool, HR software, a CRM, a project tool, chat threads and someone's memory. None of them talk to each other, and each one fits about 70% of how you actually work.

Acuolo Studio builds one system that runs how your company works, with people, clients, projects and process connected in one source of truth. It is built around your process, runs in your own cloud, and you own it, with no per-seat fees.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

What HR templates does Acuolo offer? +

Acuolo has five tools live right now, all free to use with no account: an HR diagnostic that tells you what to fix first, a custom performance evaluation form builder, a career ladder framework builder, a recruiting framework builder and an engagement survey builder. Each generates a finished document from your answers, which you can copy and edit. Alongside them, Acuolo Studio builds a custom company operating system around how your company works, quoted per project. More products are in development.

How do I get access to the tools? +

The six live tools are free and need no account or payment — open one, answer the questions, copy the document it produces. For Acuolo Studio, our custom build service, you either book a discovery call or fill in the intake form and we come back with a scope and a quote.

How much do Acuolo HR templates cost? +

The five tools currently live are free. Listed prices for products still in development are indicative and nothing is purchasable on the site yet. Acuolo Studio, our custom build service, is quoted per project after a discovery call.

What format do I get the document in? +

Each tool builds the document in the page and gives you a copy button that puts it on your clipboard as Markdown. Paste it into Notion, Google Docs, Word or your survey tool and format it however you like. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

Do you offer consulting for companies that need custom HR systems? +

Yes — that is Acuolo Studio. Instead of a document you edit yourself, we build the people system itself around how your company actually works, engineered by the team at Fakir Technology Consultants. It is scoped and quoted per project after a discovery call. Tell us what you need through the intake form, or book a call.
